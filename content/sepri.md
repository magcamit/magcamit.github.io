The SEPRI Consortium brings together academics and researchers from four research-intensive and impact-driven universities – the University of Manchester, Hiroshima University, Osaka University, and the University of Tokyo – in forming a dynamic and collaborative platform dedicated to fostering just, inclusive, and equitable local communities as a foundation for sustainable everyday peace.

By integrating scholarly, policy, and technical expertise, our Consortium aims to advance locally attuned, diverse, and inclusive conceptions and practices of everyday peacemaking. Central to this mission is the commitment to foreground the lived experiences, differentiated vulnerabilities, and peace aspirations of communities across Manchester, Hiroshima, Osaka, and Tokyo in all our research and impact-related activities and engagements such as through this event.
