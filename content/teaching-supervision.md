I am keen on supervising prospective PhD students interested in doing research on the following thematic subjects:

- Emotions, Ethnoreligious Conflicts, and Affective Peacebuilding
- (in)Security Politics of International Trade
- Populism, Securitisation, and Foreign Policy
- Decolonial Southeast Asian Politics and IR

I will be particularly interested in reading proposals that directly engage with or at least speak to my work just so I can have a much clearer idea as to how I might be able to help and support you and your scholarship.
